Exports require the admin role — taking the dataset out of the platform is a different act from reading a report. Every export is recorded in the audit log before a row is sent.

What is deliberately not in the file: device and IP hashes. They are ours rather than yours — derived from an advertising ID and an address under a system-wide pepper, which makes them stable pseudonyms for a person. Your own anonymous_id and user_id are included.

Once downloaded, a file is outside the reach of any later deletion request. Ranges are capped at 31 days and one million rows, and both are refusals rather than truncations — a silently short CSV is worse than one that fails.
